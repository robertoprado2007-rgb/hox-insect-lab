What if a fly had four wings?

Genes called Hox genes are like a tiny set of instructions that say what grows where on an insect. Switch one off and see what happens!

Learn Play in the Lab For Teachers

How it works

1

Pick an insect

Start with a fruit fly, or choose a beetle, a bee or a butterfly.

2

Switch genes on or off

Each Hox gene helps decide what a body part becomes. Flip them and watch.

3

See the new body

The 3D insect changes right away. Spin it around and look closely.
